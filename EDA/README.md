# EXPLORATORY DATA ANALYSIS REPORT

## OVERVIEW
This project focuses on Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), which involves examining a dataset to understand its main characteristics, patterns, trends, and distributions before carrying out more advanced analysis. The analysis provides an opportunity to investigate the data systematically and identify meaningful observations that may not be immediately visible from the raw dataset.
The analysis includes calculating basic descriptive statistics such as count, mean, and median to understand the distribution and typical values of key numerical variables. The dataset will also be examined for trends and unusual values that may indicate potential outliers. These findings will be used to highlight important patterns within the data and provide a clearer understanding of the relationships and behaviours represented in the dataset.
The project also focuses on interpreting the results rather than simply presenting calculations. By examining the data from different perspectives, the analysis aims to identify key observations, unusual patterns, and areas that may require further investigation. The findings from this exploratory stage will provide a foundation for making data-driven conclusions and support more advanced analysis in subsequent projects.

 ## EXPLORATORY DATA ANALYSIS
 
- ### Data Quality
The dataset contains 1,200 orders across 14 columns, covering January 2023 to June 2025. It includes 1,189 unique customers and 1,200 unique tracking numbers, with no duplicate rows or Order IDs. The dataset is largely complete, with 309 missing CouponCode values (25.75%) as the only missing data.

- ### Descriptive Analysis
The Data Analysis ToolPak's Descriptive Statistics tool was run on the four numerical fields (Quantity, UnitPrice, TotalPrice, and ItemsInCart) to get a quick statistical profile of the dataset (n = 1,200 records) before moving into deeper analysis.

Four measures were used to summarize each variable:
| Measures | Meaning |
| -- | -- |
| Mean (average)| the typical value across all 1,200 orders|
| Median (midpoint) | the value that splits the data in half, useful for checking whether the mean is being pulled up or down by outliers|
|	Min / Max (range boundaries) | the lowest and highest recorded values, showing the spread of realistic values in the data|

- ### Outlier Analysis

Outliers in TotalPrice were identified using the Interquartile Range (IQR) method. The first and third quartiles were calculated using QUARTILE.INC, and the IQR was calculated as the difference between Q3 and Q1. The lower and upper boundaries were then calculated as -1,341.41 and 3,330.41, respectively. A separate flag column was created to identify values outside these boundaries.

The analysis identified 8 outliers in TotalPrice. These were reviewed and appear to be valid high-value orders rather than data errors, so they were retained in the dataset.

## KEY INSIGHTS

The analysis was used to move beyond simply reporting numbers and identify what the results could mean for the business. Each insight considers what happened, what the data suggests, and why it matters.

- Chair generated the highest revenue at 195,620.11, closely followed by Printer at 195,612.61, while Laptop generated 192,126.56. The small difference between these products shows that revenue was not dependent on one product alone. This gives the business a relatively strong product base, while also highlighting these top-performing products as important areas to monitor and promote.

- Cancelled orders accounted for 250 orders, while 247 orders were returned. Together, they represented 41.4% of all orders. This is a large share of transactions and may indicate issues that affect customer satisfaction, order fulfilment, or product expectations. Investigating the reasons behind these cancellations and returns could help the business reduce lost sales and improve the customer experience.

- Instagram generated the highest referral revenue at 275,285.45 and also produced the highest number of orders. This suggests that Instagram is an effective source of customer traffic within this dataset. The business could use this result to evaluate what is working on the platform and consider maintaining or expanding successful campaigns.

- Revenue decreased from 552,643.24 in 2023 to 480,235.87 in 2024. The available 2025 data recorded 231,882.85 through June, but this cannot be directly compared with the full-year figures. The decline between 2023 and 2024 suggests that the business should investigate changes in product demand, customer activity, order volume, or marketing performance that may have contributed to the reduction.

- The analysis identified 8 TotalPrice outliers using the IQR method. These orders fall outside the normal spending range but were retained because they appear to represent genuine high-value purchases rather than incorrect records. This suggests that some customers place significantly larger orders, creating an opportunity to understand what products or purchasing patterns are associated with higher-value transactions.



## RECOMMENDATIONS

Based on the insights above, the following actions could help improve business performance:

- Investigate cancelled and returned orders. Review the reasons behind these transactions to identify recurring problems with products, fulfilment, delivery, or customer expectations. Reducing unnecessary cancellations and returns could improve both revenue retention and customer satisfaction.

- Strengthen successful referral channels. Since Instagram generated the highest number of orders and referral revenue, the business should examine its successful campaigns and continue investing in strategies that attract customers through this channel.
 
- Monitor and promote strong-performing products. Chair, Printer, and Laptop were among the highest revenue-generating products. The business should monitor their demand and consider using promotions, bundles, or targeted marketing to maintain their performance.

- Investigate the decline in revenue. The reduction from 2023 to 2024 should be examined alongside order volume, product performance, referral sources, and customer behaviour to determine where performance changed and where corrective action is needed.




## LIMITATIONS

While the analysis provides useful insights, several limitations should be considered:

- The dataset contains 1,200 orders, so the findings describe the available data and may not represent all future customer behaviour.

- The dataset does not specify the currency used for UnitPrice and TotalPrice. Therefore, the financial values are presented as recorded rather than being assigned a specific currency.

- The 2025 data only covers January to June, so it cannot be fairly compared with the full-year results for 2023 and 2024.

- The analysis identifies relationships and patterns in the data, but it does not prove the exact reasons behind them. For example, Instagram generated the highest referral revenue, but the data alone cannot confirm that Instagram marketing directly caused the higher revenue.

- The outlier analysis identifies unusual values but does not automatically mean those transactions are errors. The identified TotalPrice outliers were therefore reviewed and retained.




## CONCLUSION

This analysis examined 1,200 orders to understand customer purchasing behaviour, product performance, revenue patterns, referral sources, order outcomes, and unusual transactions. The findings show that customer order sizes were generally consistent, while a few products and referral sources contributed strongly to revenue. At the same time, the high proportion of cancelled and returned orders and the decline in revenue from 2023 to 2024 highlight areas that require further investigation.

The main business value of the analysis is that it turns the raw transaction data into clear areas for action. The business can use these findings to investigate lost sales, strengthen effective marketing channels, maintain strong product performance, and better understand changes in revenue over time.
